Validation principles

  • Specificity: demonstrate that the response is attributable to the analyte in the presence of relevant matrix components.
  • Accuracy: assess closeness to an accepted reference using justified levels and replicates.
  • Precision: evaluate variability under specified repeatability or intermediate-precision conditions.
  • Linearity / range: establish suitable response behavior over a justified working range; do not rely on \(R^2\) alone.
  • LOD / LOQ: use an appropriate method and verify practical performance near the estimated limits.
  • Robustness: test justified small variations in method parameters and evaluate their impact.
